Somatic Life & Leadership Coaching

This is coaching for the whole person. We don't only think through what's happening in your life — we also pay attention to how it lives in you, so change can take root and last.

There's no fixed curriculum. You bring what's alive in your life right now, and we work with what emerges. Together, we might explore:

  • Life direction and meaning
  • Career and transitions
  • Burnout, stress, and overwhelm
  • Grief, loss, and difficult emotions
  • Boundaries and relationships
  • Feeling stuck or disconnected
  • Leadership and how you show up
  • Confidence and self-trust
  • Creativity and self-expression
  • Patterns of overwork or perfectionism
  • Difficult decisions and uncertainty
  • Reconnecting with your body and yourself

Whatever you bring, we meet it with curiosity — making space for thought, emotion, the body, and the whole of your experience.

My Approach

Coaching that includes the body

🌿

Traditional coaching tends to focus on thoughts, goals, insight, and behavior. My work includes all of that — and also pays attention to what's happening in your body and nervous system as we talk.

In a session, we might work with your thoughts and beliefs and your emotions, alongside sensation, breath, movement, impulses, and the patterns of tension or ease that surface while you describe a situation.

This isn't about the body holding some magical answer. It's that insight alone doesn't always create change. The body can offer another source of information about patterns that are hard to reach through thinking alone.

My approach draws from three traditions:

🤝 Co-Active Coaching — deep listening, powerful questions, values, perspective, choice, and partnership.

🌱 Somatic work (Somatic Experiencing–informed) — working with the body, sensation, the nervous system, and embodied patterns.

🧠 Mindfulness + the science of change — building awareness and practicing new responses so insight can become lived change.

The result is change that feels grounded and real — not just understood, but lived.

About Me

Why I Do This Work

On the outside, I had a successful career. But inside, I carried stress in my body and restlessness in my heart. My journey into mindfulness and coaching began as a way to ease that discomfort — and it became a deeper path of reconnection. Now, I help others do the same: listening inward, tending to what's been ignored, and creating lives that feel aligned, sustainable, and alive.

On the outside, my career looked successful. But inside, I carried a heaviness I couldn't explain — stress in my body, restlessness in my mind, and an ache that no amount of stretching or discipline could resolve. Chronic back pain and tension followed, and I knew something deeper was calling for attention.

The shift began when I immersed myself in somatic meditation. For the first time, I could see how my mental and emotional patterns lived in my body — irritation, anger, and even depressive thoughts weren't just states of mind, but physical imprints too. Awareness brought insight, but it wasn't enough on its own. Long-held patterns don't dissolve through insight alone.

The real transformation came when I began working with a life coach. Those conversations weren't about venting — they gave me language, clarity, and tools to understand my values and desires in a way that felt embodied and real. I learned how to interrupt old patterns in the moment, speak to myself differently, and shift how I showed up in the world.

I believe that within each of us lies an innate wisdom—a kind of inner compass. When we learn to access and trust it, it guides us toward a life that feels both true and fulfilling. My role as your life coach is to help you reconnect with that compass and build the skills to live in alignment with it. My goal isn't to create dependence—it's to empower you to become your own life coach. To know yourself deeply, to learn how to reparent the parts of you still waiting to be heard, and to live more skillfully, from the inside out.
